# Shop
Once your project is approved, you will get your books based off the hours you spent on your project. You can use these books to buy prizes from the shop, or fund hardware builds.
Some things you can get from the shop are:
- Study Related Supplies
- Electronics
- Other high Hacker Value Items
- Merch
Once you order one of these items, it will be shipped to you either from the Hack Club HQ or from a local store near you.
We will try to fulfill your orders ASAP, but it may take a while depending on how many orders we have. If we are not
able to get you your item, we will give you a grant to buy the item yourself. As well as this, depending on the item,
we may adjust prices based on the current market price, which will not affect you if you already ordered the item but if you
are planning to, just keep that in mind.
