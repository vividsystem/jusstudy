# Sourcing Parts
When you make a hardware project, you have to make a BOM (A bill of materials), so we know how much to give you as a grant.
This BOM will have the parts and prices you need to build your project (this does not include 3D Printing costs or PCB costs.)

## What Parts do I get?
When you are looking for parts, you want to find parts that are cheap, and do what you need. Please do not try to get parts just
because they are expensive could be cool to have, you can add them in a later ship if it is required. As Hack Club is a nonprofit
we cannot afford to fund projects where you need a 30 inch touchscreen for a temperature sensing arduino device.

## Where do I get them from?
I would recommend AliExpress to look for parts because
- They normally have free or cheap shipping
- They have cheap parts that work most of the time
- They allow returns if the parts do not work
A few things about AliExpress to be aware about is that it can take 2-6 weeks depending on where you are, to get your parts, some parts such as lithium batteries cannot be shipped internationally, and some parts can be mislabeled or counterfeit. These are some things to be aware of when looking for parts.
If you cannot find the parts there, find a retailer with a low price, which you can either get delivered for cheap or pick up locally.
If you do not know where to get the parts from, feel free to send a message in our Slack channel and someone will find something for you.
